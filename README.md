# 🎮 Roll and Rush

**Roll and Rush** is a 3D game developed with **Unity** and **C#**.

The player controls a sphere that can roll in four directions, jump, collect points, interact with obstacles, and take damage from enemies and environmental hazards.

🚧 **This project is currently in early development.**

The game is being developed from scratch as a learning and game-development project, with new gameplay systems and features being added progressively.

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay

The player controls a rolling sphere and must navigate through the game environment while collecting points and avoiding hazards.

### Player abilities

* ⬆️ Move forward
* ⬇️ Move backward
* ⬅️ Move left
* ➡️ Move right
* 🦘 Jump
* 🔄 Roll while moving
* ⭐ Collect points
* ❤️ Take damage
* 💀 Interact with enemies and obstacles
